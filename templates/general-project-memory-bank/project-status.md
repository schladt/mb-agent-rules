## Internal

Execution-status source only. Keep private detail here, never in publishable
sections. Findings validity, custody, consent and scope retain their own authority.

Publishable means approved for teammates without memory-bank access, not public
or client delivery permission. The five sections generate root team-status.md
with the heading "# Team Status"; this source and its receipt remain private.
Client-facing reports/exports belong in deliverables/ and client updates in the
project's separate client update file, not team-status.md. The --external-status
option and external_status metadata mean team output outside the bank.

Replace "Not yet reported" only with supported facts; leaving an unknown explicit
is better than inventing progress. Keep this writing guidance in Internal.
Preserve the exact ordered headings and five nonempty Publishable sections;
do not add subheadings or code fences there. Use bold workstream labels with
nested bullets or tables to organize substantive, self-contained updates:

- Progress: explain context, scope/coverage, work performed and concrete outcomes.
  Explain references in ordinary language; private links, raw evidence and opaque
  identifiers cannot be the only explanation.
- Milestones: give supported achievements or targets, known owners and dates,
  distinguishing completed work from plans and explicit unknowns.
- Blockers: explain the affected work, impact, known owner and unblocking steps.
- Next Steps: prioritize actionable work, with known owners, dates and dependencies.
- Client Actions: describe the client's needed decisions, inputs or access and
  what they unblock; include known owners/dates or state that these are unknown.

Never fabricate completion, certainty, ownership or dates. Dates describe sourced
facts rather than generation time. Run workflow status after source edits when
team output is enabled; report errors/staleness and retain prior valid output.
Warnings do not prove sanitization or grant sharing permission.
Describe results and context in prose even when supplying links. Links must be
team-accessible and resolve relative to root team-status.md, not the bank source.
Do not require .memory-bank/ links or artifact IDs as context: generation depends
on the private authority; readership does not.
